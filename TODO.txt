### 12. Other

I think *Other should deliberately be a proper category* rather than us continuing to invent increasingly specific top-level categories.

If something clearly belongs on Blissbase but doesn't have a natural home yet, it goes into Other. We can then periodically analyze what accumulates there. If we start seeing a significant cluster around a particular area, the actual event data can tell us when another category deserves to exist.

### One potential additional category to discuss: Creativity & Expression

This is the one area where I think there is a genuine strategic question.

Hipsy has a meaningful amount of creative content that overlaps with the personal-development audience. So should we potentially add:

*Creativity & Expression*

with an underlying vocabulary such as:

•⁠  ⁠Expressive Arts
•⁠  ⁠Intuitive Painting
•⁠  ⁠Creative Writing
•⁠  ⁠Poetry
•⁠  ⁠Storytelling
•⁠  ⁠Theatre / Improvisation
•⁠  ⁠Conscious Creativity
•⁠  ⁠Therapeutic Art

I wouldn't want Blissbase to become a place for generic painting classes, photography courses or arts & crafts.

The question is more specifically whether we want to include *creative expression when creativity itself is being used as a pathway to self-exploration, personal development or transformation*.

I haven't included it in the 11 core categories above because I think that's a positioning decision we should make together rather than simply adding another category. (i’d not include ti at his stage)
